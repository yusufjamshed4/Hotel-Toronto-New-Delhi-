Why Choose Hotel Toronto?

If you are looking for an economical, no-nonsense stay close to Connaught Place and New Delhi Railway Station without burning a hole in your pocket, Hotel Toronto stands out as a reliable choice. Located safely right opposite the police station, it offers a welcoming environment for families, corporate travelers, and tourists alike.

✅ Guest Highlights

  • Prime location near New Delhi Railway Station & Metro.
  • Highly budget-friendly base pricing starting around ₹734+ per night.
  • Friendly front-desk staff & flexible check-in policies.
  • Rooftop terrace offering wonderful panoramic city views.

⚠️ Good to Know

  • Standard rooms are compact; consider upgrading to AC Back Side variants.
  • Outside food is not permitted inside rooms (in-house multi-cuisine dining available).

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Standard check-in begins at 12:00 PM and check-out is until 12:00 PM.

Are unmarried couples allowed?

Yes, unmarried couples, local ID holders, and all-male groups are explicitly permitted. Guests must be 18+.

Is parking available on-site?

Yes, free self-parking and free valet parking options are available for guests.

Are pets allowed inside the hotel?

No, pets are strictly prohibited within the hotel premises.
